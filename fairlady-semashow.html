Drivers
JDM ¥

Two overlapping forces: the ongoing JDM boom and the weak yen, which makes good Japanese examples look like undervalued gems to overseas buyers.

src: projectjdm.org · 2026 JDM market outlook
01 · A market starting to split

Toward 2030 the market shifts from "any S30 is valuable" to a condition-driven split. Highly original or fully restored cars are treated as cultural assets; rusty, modified cars hold value mostly as restoration bases or donor stock.

02 · What this means for body panels

As good shells are restored and parted out, demand for fresh structural and cosmetic panels — floors, sills, rails, hoods, tail panels — follows. That's exactly the catalogue we tool in-house.
