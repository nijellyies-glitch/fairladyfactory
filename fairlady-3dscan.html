How ordering works

Tell us what you need

From a worn original to a one-of-one piece — a simple five-step process. No catalogue, no minimum run, no waiting on a part that no longer exists.

This is the workflow we follow See it in action — Triforge 3D scanning & printing an automotive headlight (Facebook). The same pipeline, tuned for classic-car parts.
↗
01

Send it

Send us the part you need — a photo, the broken piece, a moulding, a bezel, or even just a drawing. If it's physical, ship it; if it's gone, describe it.

02

Scan it

We 3D-scan the original to capture every curve, clip and screw boss — the step Triforge 3D show on the headlight. Nothing is guessed; it's measured.

03

Model it

The scan becomes clean, manufacturable CAD — a digital twin that fits the first time. You approve the model before anything is printed.

04

Print it

3D-printed one at a time — a unique piece made just for you, in the material your part calls for. No moulds, no minimums.

05

Fit it

Finished, painted, ready to install. A part that bolts on first time, every time — shipped to your door.
